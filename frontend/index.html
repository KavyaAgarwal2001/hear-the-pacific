How it maps

  • Pitch — the Oceanic Niño Index (3-month mean Niño-3.4 anomaly) mapped linearly from −3 °C to +3 °C onto a three-octave pentatonic scale. Higher is always warmer.
  • Timbre — warm, cool and neutral seasons each have their own voice, using NOAA's ±0.5 °C thresholds.
  • Drone — monthly CO₂ at Mauna Loa opens a filter as concentrations rise. It enters in 1958, when the record begins.

Limitations

  • Pitch is rounded to a scale, so seasons within ~0.2 °C can share a note. Hover the chart for exact values.
  • A single warm or cool season is not an El Niño or La Niña episode; NOAA requires five consecutive overlapping seasons.
  • The most recent values are preliminary and NOAA revises past values when base periods update.
  • The audio shows patterns. Use the numbers, not the sound, for any quantitative claim.
